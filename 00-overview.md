## Study tip

Read a chapter's `## Key concepts`, cover the `## Cheatsheet` and try to recall it, then do the `## Quick self-check`. Anything you miss, re-read that concept or rewatch just that lecture. To revise fast before an interview, read only the Cheatsheets and Common Mistakes across all chapters.
